## 🛰️ About Me

<img align="right" width="220" src="https://rapeepat.com/assets/photos/me-portrait.webp" alt="Rapeepat" />

I'm **Rapeepat Jaroensri (Yu)**, a **Computer Engineering** student in Bangkok, Thailand.

I like building things that leave the screen and work in the real world — from **drone swarms flying in formation**, to **tool-using AI agents**, to **embedded hardware** and **games**.

- 🚁 **Drones:** multi-drone swarm control, formation flight, MAVLink, live video
- 🤖 **AI:** voice AI agents, LLM tool use, computer vision / object detection
- 🔌 **Embedded:** ESP32-S3, round displays, firmware & sensors, MQTT
- 🎮 **Games:** multiplayer browser games, gameplay systems, prototypes
- 🧑‍🏫 **Also:** assistant instructor & staff at STEM / AIoT youth workshops
